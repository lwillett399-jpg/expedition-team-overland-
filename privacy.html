Privacy Policy

Last updated August 18, 2026.

Overview

Expedition Team Overland ("ETO," "we," "us," or "our") operates expeditionteamoverland.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect from visitors, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few limited ways:

  • Information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and message when you use our contact form.
  • Usage data collected automatically through standard analytics tools (for example, pages visited, time on site, and general location derived from IP address), which helps us understand how the Site is used.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used by embedded third-party content, such as YouTube video players, which may set their own cookies subject to that provider's privacy policy.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to respond to messages sent through the contact form, understand and improve the Site, and maintain the security of the Site. We do not sell your personal information.

Affiliate Links & Sponsored Content

Expedition Team Overland participates in affiliate programs, including with partners featured on this Site. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Product recommendations reflect our own experience on the trail; affiliate relationships do not influence which products we choose to feature.

Third-Party Links & Embedded Content

The Site links to and embeds content from third-party services, including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, and our partners' own websites. These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of sites we don't operate.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the Site, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact Us
