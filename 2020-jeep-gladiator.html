Purpose Built

A Complete Expedition Platform

This isn't just a Gladiator. It's a fully self-contained overland vehicle designed for extended off-grid travel without compromise.

Starting with a Sting Gray 2020 Jeep Gladiator Rubicon, every component was selected to balance reliability, trail capability, comfort and self-sufficiency. More than $100,000 in premium upgrades support everything from weekend escapes to multi-week trips across North America.
