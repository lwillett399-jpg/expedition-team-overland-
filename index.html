Who Is ETO

It's About the Journey, Not the Destination

I'm Robert Frank, founder of Expedition Team Overland: a family-focused adventure project built around exploring North America by vehicle and documenting the places, stories, and history we find along the way.

We're not chasing the most extreme trip possible. Our philosophy is simple: explore farther, find interesting places, learn their stories, and enjoy the journey getting there.
