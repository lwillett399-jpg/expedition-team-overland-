Who Is ETO

Built From Real Trail Miles

We're Expedition Team Overland, a crew that's spent more weekends broken down on a fire road than we'd like to admit. Since 2017 we've been out there documenting real overland travel: what breaks, what works, and what we'd do differently next time.

No sponsored fantasy, just field notes from the trail. Follow along and get inspired to plan your own adventure.
